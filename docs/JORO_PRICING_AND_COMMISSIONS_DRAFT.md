# JORO Pricing & Commission Policy — revised working draft

Source: `01_JORO_Pricing_and_Commissions.docx`, version 1.0, updated 13 October 2026. This local revision records the combined Delivery Charges decision. The source Word file is unchanged. This draft is **not approved for publication** while seller commission and settlement terms remain unresolved.

## What customers pay

The admin-configured Delivery Charges are the single customer charge for delivery on standard product checkout. They may include courier, fulfilment and an internal Rs. 50 handling component where applicable. JORO does **not** add a separate customer Handling Fee. If Delivery Charges are zero or below Rs. 50, checkout keeps that configured amount; JORO bears any uncovered internal handling cost. The amount shown before confirmation is product subtotal plus booked services and Delivery Charges, less discounts, plus any lawfully applicable and disclosed taxes. Negotiated quote shipping retains the quoted amount. This rule creates no new fee for standalone services or other checkout types.

Returns and refunds remain governed by the applicable approved policy, transaction terms and law. This draft makes no new non-refundable fee rule.

## Seller and service-provider commission — decision pending

The source document proposes seller rates of 3.5% for mobiles/electronics, 4% for furniture/home appliances, 5% for general products, 6% for gifts/personalized goods and fashion/beauty, and 8% for standalone and add-on services. It also proposes a Rs. 3,000 cap for eligible electronics seller-orders during an initial 90-day pilot. **These proposed rates and cap have not been verified against live settings or contracts and are not asserted as currently charged.**

Current code records **zero vendor commission on standard product orders** and pays vendors the item sale amount after any applicable vendor-funded discount. Negotiated quote orders retain vendor-deducted commission from the category/vendor rate resolver. Add-on service commissions use separate service ranges. The combined delivery presentation does not change those settlement calculations.

The owner must decide whether the proposed product commission table applies to any future standard orders and reconcile seller agreements, promotional caps, cancellation adjustments and settlement statements. No seller commission should be newly charged merely because this draft mentions a rate table.

## Illustrations requiring revision

The source's Rs. 250 courier plus Rs. 50 fulfilment example describes one **Rs. 300 Delivery Charges** line for that example only. It is not a universal delivery price. A Rs. 2,000 product with admin-configured Rs. 200 delivery totals Rs. 2,200 before discounts and taxes; Rs. 50 is not added. Configured free delivery totals Rs. 2,000. The source's product-plus-installation example requires review against the actual configured delivery amount and service commission settings before publication.

Confirmed historical orders retain their stored totals and fees. New pricing takes effect only for orders created after deployment. The owner must set the effective publication date and any customer or seller notice.
