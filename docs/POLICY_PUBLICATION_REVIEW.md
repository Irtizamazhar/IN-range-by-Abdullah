# JORO policy publication review — working notes

The local Terms page and Pricing & Commission Policy draft are working copies. The three supplied Word drafts remain unchanged and require owner and legal review before publication. The Returns and Refunds draft was not changed by the delivery work. Do not infer a new non-refundable fee rule.

## Approved combined delivery rule

For new standard product orders, `settings.codCharges` remains the customer Delivery Charges amount. Where applicable, this amount includes fulfilment and an internal Rs. 50 handling component. Checkout does not add a separate customer Handling Fee. A configured delivery charge of zero stays zero; a charge below Rs. 50 is not raised. JORO bears any uncovered internal handling cost. Negotiated quote shipping stays as quoted. The single Delivery Charges line and final total are disclosed before confirmation.

Older orders retain their stored `Order.handlingFee`, `totalAmount` and invoice/receipt breakdown. The former customer fee was percentage based before the local Rs. 50 standalone change; the now-approved combined delivery rule supersedes that pending local change for new orders. There is no historical rewrite or double recognition of delivery and handling.

## Seller and service-provider settlement

Standard product orders currently record zero vendor commission and pay the seller the item sale amount after any vendor-funded discount. Negotiated quotes retain vendor-deducted commission. Add-on services have their own rate calculation. The combined delivery rule does not alter any of these. The source Pricing draft's proposed seller rate table, electronics pilot cap and service terms still require a separate decision against live settings, contracts and settlement statements.

4. Resolve remaining placeholders, effective date, contact information and legal approval before publishing any policy.
